## 1. PURPOSE

The Parties may disclose certain confidential information to one another in connection with discussions regarding potential collaboration, business opportunities, technology integration, or other projects (the "Purpose"). The Parties wish to protect the confidentiality of such information.

---

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether in writing, orally, visually, electronically, or by observation, including but not limited to:

- Business plans, strategies, and financial data;
- Technical data, source code, algorithms, models, and specifications;
- Customer and vendor lists;
- Marketing plans and pricing;
- Unreleased product designs and roadmaps;
- Any information marked "Confidential" or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the disclosure and the circumstances.

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

The Receiving Party agrees to:

- Hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence;
- Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
- Disclose Confidential Information only to employees, contractors, or advisors who have a need to know and are bound by written obligations no less protective than this Agreement;
- Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent;
- Protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information (but no less than reasonable care);
- Promptly notify the Disclosing Party of any unauthorized use or disclosure.

---

## 4. EXCLUSIONS

The obligations under this Agreement do not apply to information that:

- Is or becomes publicly available through lawful means;
- Was in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure;
- Is received from a third party without breach of any obligation of confidentiality;
- Is independently developed without reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information;
- Is required to be disclosed by law, court order, or governmental agency, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order and discloses only what is legally required.
